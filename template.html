Your bets are saved on this device only. To check closing prices and results, press Copy my bets, save the text as a file named bets.json next to edge_scanner.py on your computer, and run python edge_scanner.py --settle-bets bets.json. Then paste the contents of the file it writes (bets_settled.json) into the box and press Load from box. The same box backs up your bets and restores them on another device.

Min odds is the lowest decimal price worth taking at your book: (1 + minimum edge) ÷ true probability. Use it only on the same market and line, such as the same total or spread number.

True probability is the price with its margin removed (). It's an estimate, not a certainty.

Stake is fractional Kelly sized at the min odds and capped. Better odds than the min mean more edge than you asked for.

Top picks shows the best flagged pick in each game, ranked by a score: the estimated edge at the low end of the margin, scaled down when confidence is lower. Only one pick per game, because picks from the same game are correlated, so betting several is closer to one bigger bet. Confidence drops a level for each of these: a sport not yet measured against your book, sharp books that disagree by more than 3 points, corners or both-teams-to-score markets, and games more than 3 days away.
