Min odds is the lowest decimal price worth taking at your book: (1 + minimum edge) ÷ true probability. Use it only on the same market and line, such as the same total or spread number.

True probability is the price with its margin removed (). It's an estimate, not a certainty.

Stake is fractional Kelly sized at the min odds and capped. Better odds than the min mean more edge than you asked for.
